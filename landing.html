About PopQuizzer

PopQuizzer is a fast, interactive quiz platform designed for learning, competitions, and fun. It transforms ordinary questions into exciting real-time experiences that engage both creators and players.

Why PopQuizzer?

⚡ Real-time gameplay

Questions, answers, and scores update instantly for all players, making every quiz fast, interactive, and exciting.

🏆 Live leaderboard

Rankings change live as players answer questions, creating healthy competition and boosting engagement.

⏱ Timed questions

Each question has a countdown timer that keeps the game dynamic and encourages quick thinking.

📊 Smart results

Get clear insights with final scores, correct answers, and performance breakdowns after every quiz.

🎨 Clean UI

A distraction-free, modern interface that is easy to use for both quiz creators and players.

📱 Mobile friendly

Fully responsive design that works smoothly on mobiles, tablets, and desktops—play anywhere.
